Industry material is not cited here

The International Sweeteners Association, the Calorie Control Council, UNESDA and the beverage manufacturers are all industry bodies, and nothing on this site rests on their publications. Note that "aspartame is one of the most studied food additives" is their standard line as much as the FDA's — volume of research is not a verdict, and the argument belongs on what the studies found rather than how many there are.

Where a source could not be retrieved or verified, the site says so rather than paraphrasing around the gap.
